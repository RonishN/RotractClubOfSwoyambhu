# DESIGN.md

## Brand Identity & Aesthetic
Neo Kinpaku Nepalese Heritage: warm saffron gold accent on rich dark navy and authentic Lokta paper textures. Restraint in layout, prestige in detail.

## Tokens & Color Palette
- **Primary Accent (`--saffron`)**: `#FF8A00` / `oklch(70% 0.20 55)`
- **Secondary Accent (`--gold`)**: `#FFD13B`
- **Surface Navy (`--navy`)**: `#1C2B4C`
- **Background (`.lokta-texture`)**: Custom Nepalese Lokta textured paper background `#FCFBF7`
- **Text Body**: `#475569`
- **Text Headings**: `#1C2B4C`

## Typography
- **Headings**: Serif / EnglishFontBold / NepaliFont
- **Body**: System Sans / Inter / NepaliFont

## Components
- **Card**: Full-width horizontal rectangle cards with left image carousel, rounded 24px borders, dynamic hover translateY(-4px) and subtle depth shadow.
- **Button**: Saffron gradient pill buttons with directional arrow indicators.
